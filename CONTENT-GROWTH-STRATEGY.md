## Publishing controls

1. Check the existing article archive and Search Console query data before commissioning a topic; avoid multiple URLs answering the same query intent.
2. Verify release dates, episode/chapter ranges, adaptation status, regional streaming availability, bylines, and publication/update dates from primary or authoritative sources.
3. Keep time-sensitive material visibly dated and revise the page only when the underlying facts change.
4. Link to real, canonical Animoro routes; do not create placeholder detail pages or fabricated internal links.
5. Add an editor, sources, and a distinct reader benefit before publishing API-dependent content.

Our Story

About Animoro

Your Daily Dose of Anime

Animoro was founded with a singular conviction: anime deserves criticism and journalism that matches the boundless artistic ambition of the medium itself.

From the sakuga mastery of Studio MAPPA and ufotable to the intimate character studies of Kyoto Animation, Japanese animation has evolved into a global cultural titan. Yet much of contemporary coverage remains shallow, cluttered with sensationalist clickbait and automated summaries. Animoro is the antidote.

What We Stand For

  • Deep Editorial Integrity: Every review, tier list, and retrospective is authored with rigorous attention to narrative structure, thematic nuance, and visual direction.
  • No Sensationalist Noise: We report confirmed production news, verifiable broadcast dates, and genuine creator interviews.
  • Comprehensive Guides: Whether you are diving into the labyrinthine Fate universe or seeking the chronological watch order for Monogatari, our guides are meticulously curated.
  • Reader-Powered Community: All article view metrics, trending algorithms, and discussion points are powered by real readers engaging with real journalism.

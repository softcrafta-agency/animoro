## Cannibalization assessment

- **`/anime.html` vs `/anime-list`:** not two competing pages in production. `/anime.html` is a permanent redirect to `/anime-list`; keep one canonical catalog.
- **`/anime-calendar.html` vs `/anime-list`:** different intents. The catalog searches and filters titles; the calendar lists weekly broadcasts and separately editor-published episode schedules. Keep both and cross-link with descriptive anchors.
- **Homepage airing section vs calendar:** the homepage currently has no airing section, as previously requested. There is no homepage schedule block to cannibalize the calendar.
- **Villain ranking vs character-power ranking:** some broad “strongest/powerful anime” wording overlaps, but the villain-only page and all-character page have different subjects and lists. Keep separate; make the page titles/intros explicitly distinguish the scope.
- **“Anime like” articles:** Solo Leveling, Naruto, and Attack on Titan pages serve different franchise-specific recommendation intents. Keep separate; require genuinely different selection criteria and examples.
